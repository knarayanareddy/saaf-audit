# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Annex A.8.8 Management of technical vulnerabilities

**Control:** Information about technical vulnerabilities of information systems in use shall be obtained, the organisation's exposure to such vulnerabilities shall be evaluated and appropriate measures shall be taken.

**Purpose:** To prevent exploitation of technical vulnerabilities.

**Guidance (ISO 27002):** The organization should:
- maintain an inventory of assets to identify which technical vulnerabilities apply;
- obtain information about technical vulnerabilities of these assets in a timely manner;
- evaluate the organization's exposure to such vulnerabilities;
- take appropriate measures to address the associated risk (e.g. patching, hardening, compensating controls);
- define and communicate roles and responsibilities for vulnerability management;
- establish timelines to react to vulnerabilities, prioritised by risk.

**Timelines:** ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A.8.8 does NOT prescribe a specific numeric threshold such as "30 days" or "90 days". The standard uses the term **"timely"** and **"appropriate measures"** — the specific SLA must be defined by the organisation based on risk. Internal policies commonly use 30/60/90-day targets for Critical/High/Medium vulnerabilities, but those thresholds are organisational, not ISO-mandated.
