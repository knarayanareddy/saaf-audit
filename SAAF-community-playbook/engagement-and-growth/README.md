# Engagement And Growth

How members move from "attended once" to "champion." How participation
gets rebuilt when it drops. How the chapter's communication holds the
community together between sessions.

The vocabulary in this section — advocacy funnel, engagement decay,
rebuilding through micro-actions — is the standard frame used in
community work generally, adapted to the SAAF context.

## In this section

- **[advocacy-funnel.md](advocacy-funnel.md)** — The four-stage
  progression from participant to champion, the signals at each
  stage, and what the chapter does at each stage to keep people
  moving.
- **[recognition-and-rituals.md](recognition-and-rituals.md)** —
  Naming contributors, leaderboards (with caution), the "thanks
  email" practice, and the rituals that mark contribution without
  turning it into a points game.
- **[rebuilding-participation.md](rebuilding-participation.md)** —
  When attendance or contribution drops. How to recognise it, how to
  reach out, and how to bring people back without pretending the
  decline didn't happen.
- **[communication-channels.md](communication-channels.md)** — One
  channel, one mailing list, one calendar. Why more is worse.

## Reading order

Read [advocacy-funnel.md](advocacy-funnel.md) first; the rest of the
section depends on its vocabulary.
