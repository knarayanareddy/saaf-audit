# Foundations

The shared bedrock under every SAAF chapter — internal or regional.

Read this section before you read either of the chapter playbooks. It
explains *what* you are starting, *why* it works, and the conditions
under which it will not work. Skipping it and going straight to "how do
I run a hackathon" tends to produce a hackathon that looks like SAAF and
isn't.

## In this section

- **[what-saaf-is.md](what-saaf-is.md)** — The four pillars, the PDCA
  cycle, the role split, and the difference between SAAF and a working
  group, an industry consortium, or a vendor user group.
- **[principles.md](principles.md)** — Ten operating principles, written
  for chapter founders. These are the practical extensions of the
  [manifesto](../MANIFESTO.md).
- **[when-to-start.md](when-to-start.md)** — Honest signals that a
  chapter will work, signals it will fail, and three specific
  anti-patterns to avoid.

When you are done with this section, you should be able to answer:

1. What does my chapter exist to do that is not already done elsewhere?
2. Who needs to say yes for this to work?
3. What would make me shut it down within twelve months?

If any of those answers are vague, stop and resolve them before
recruiting champions or booking a venue.
