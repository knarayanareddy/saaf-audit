# Day-Of Checklist

The hour-by-hour list for the operator and the captain on session
day. Two halves: morning (keynotes and presentations) and afternoon
(the hackathon, 13:00–17:00).

---

## 07:00 — Operator Arrival

- [ ] Doors unlocked, lights on.
- [ ] Heating / cooling adjusted.
- [ ] Wifi tested by walking the room.
- [ ] AV tested with operator's laptop.
- [ ] Tables in correct configuration.
- [ ] Extension strips placed on every table.
- [ ] Table signs placed.
- [ ] Manifesto placed on the back of each table sign.
- [ ] Wifi password sign on each table.
- [ ] Welcome / agenda card on each table.

## 07:30 — Captain Arrival

- [ ] Walk the room with the operator. Confirm everything above.
- [ ] Brief the volunteers: door, registration, floater.
- [ ] Walk through the day's agenda once with volunteers.
- [ ] Test the demo screen and adapters.
- [ ] Quiet room / retreat space signposted.

## 08:00 — Catering Arrival

- [ ] Catering team arrives with the agreed setup time.
- [ ] Allergen labels visible on every dish.
- [ ] Hot drinks station ready.
- [ ] Cups / crockery available.
- [ ] Catering team has dietary needs list.
- [ ] Catering team knows refreshment times (09:45, 11:00, 12:00,
      14:30, 15:30, 17:00).

## 08:30 — Doors Open

- [ ] Coffee, tea, water, light breakfast available.
- [ ] Door / registration volunteer at the entrance with attendee
      list and badges.
- [ ] Captain or co-captain at the door greeting people by name
      where possible.
- [ ] First-time attendees flagged and welcomed.
- [ ] Photo-consent stickers offered at the registration desk.

---

## Morning — Keynotes And Presentations

### 09:00 — Welcome (15 min)

- [ ] Captain delivers the welcome (manifesto principle, today's
      shape, practicalities).
- [ ] Captain names the morning/afternoon split explicitly: talks
      now, hackathon at 13:00, drinks at 17:00 sharp.
- [ ] First-timers asked to raise hands and welcomed.
- [ ] Captain ends within 15 minutes.

### 09:15 — Keynote (30 min)

- [ ] Visible timer started.
- [ ] Captain stands at the back ready to signal at 5-minute
      remaining.
- [ ] Captain ends the slot at 30 minutes regardless.
- [ ] Captain thanks the speaker briefly and moves on.

### 09:45 — Coffee Break (15 min)

- [ ] Fresh coffee, water, light snacks.
- [ ] Operator visible at the catering setup.
- [ ] Captain mingles, takes the temperature of the room.

### 10:00 — Presentation 1 (30 min)

- [ ] Visible timer.
- [ ] Captain enforces the hard cap.

### 10:30 — Presentation 2 (30 min)

- [ ] Visible timer.
- [ ] Captain enforces the hard cap.

### 11:00 — Coffee Break (15 min)

- [ ] Refresh coffee, water, snacks.
- [ ] Captain confirms with track leads which plans will be walked
      through and in what order.

### 11:15 — Plan Walkthrough + Claim Moment (45 min)

- [ ] Captain or track lead walks through each plan.
- [ ] Each plan's track and table location stated.
- [ ] Claim moment announced clearly — teams form during lunch.
- [ ] Captain available at the front for "I don't have a team yet"
      participants.

### 12:00 — Lunch (60 min)

- [ ] Lunch served all at once if possible (or in two waves).
- [ ] All allergens labelled visibly.
- [ ] Table signs make team/plan locations findable.
- [ ] Captain moves between tables; helps teams that haven't yet
      formed.

---

## Afternoon — The Hackathon (13:00–17:00)

### 13:00 — Hackathon Kickoff + Build Sprint 1 (90 min)

- [ ] Captain announces, on the dot: *"The hackathon starts now."*
- [ ] Captain walks every team in the first 30 minutes.
- [ ] Track leads walking their tracks.
- [ ] Notes taken on stuck teams for the coffee-break check-in.
- [ ] Recap writer gathering one-paragraph "what we're building"
      from each team.

### 14:30 — Coffee Break + Roaming Check-Ins (15 min)

- [ ] Fresh coffee, water, snacks available.
- [ ] Captain visits the stuck teams. Get them unstuck or re-scope.
- [ ] Operator checks the catering setup is replenished.

### 14:45 — Build Sprint 2 (75 min)

- [ ] Captain walks the room.
- [ ] Cross-track collaboration spotted and encouraged.
- [ ] At 15:30, snack table refreshed.
- [ ] At 15:45, captain announces "fifteen minutes until demos" to
      focus teams.

### 16:00 — Demo Session (45 min)

- [ ] Visible timer.
- [ ] 5 minutes per team, hard cap.
- [ ] Captain runs the timer and is willing to cut.
- [ ] Recap writer taking detailed notes.
- [ ] Demos recorded with consent (if planned).

### 16:45 — Closing (15 min)

- [ ] Captain thanks the host organisation, speakers, volunteers,
      operator.
- [ ] Captain names every team and every plan claimed today.
- [ ] PDCA status update for each plan, one line each.
- [ ] Captain announces date and venue of next session.
- [ ] Captain reads one sentence from the manifesto.
- [ ] Quick survey to participants (5 questions max).
- [ ] Captain ends at 17:00 on the dot.

### 17:00 — Drinks (sharp start)

- [ ] Drinks and light food set up.
- [ ] Captain and founding team circulating, not clustering.
- [ ] Operator visible in case anything goes wrong.
- [ ] End time announced (typically 19:00 or 19:30).

### 19:00 — Close-Down

- [ ] Final guests escorted out.
- [ ] All laptops, chargers, stationery returned to owners.
- [ ] Room left in agreed condition.
- [ ] Photos and demo recordings collected.
- [ ] Operator's bag re-packed.
- [ ] Operator and captain confirm everything is done.
- [ ] Captain goes home.

---

## When Things Go Wrong

For specific scenarios (wifi failure, speaker no-show, vendor
incident), see [running-hackathons/facilitation.md](../../running-hackathons/facilitation.md).

The general rule: triage in order — people, then content, then
logistics. Delegate to the right role. Communicate to the room.

If the morning runs long, **the 17:00 close still holds**. Compress
the demo session before pushing into the evening. See
[running-hackathons/format-and-agenda.md](../../running-hackathons/format-and-agenda.md)
for the cut-time priority list.
