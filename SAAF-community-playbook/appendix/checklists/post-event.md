# Post-Event Checklist

The seven days after a session.

---

## Within 1 Hour Of Doors Closing

- [ ] All recoverable laptops, chargers, and stationery reunited
      with owners.
- [ ] Room left in the state the venue requested.
- [ ] Photos and demo recordings uploaded to the chapter's archive.
- [ ] Operator's bag re-packed and stored for next session.

## Within 24 Hours

- [ ] Thank-you note sent to the venue contact.
- [ ] Thank-you note sent to the catering contact.
- [ ] Thank-you note sent to the AV / IT contact at the venue.
- [ ] Thank-you note sent to the keynote speaker (personal, by the
      captain).
- [ ] Captain's running notes updated: what worked, what didn't.

## Within 3 Days

- [ ] Recap writer's draft of the recap circulated to the captain
      and co-captain for factual corrections.
- [ ] PDCA statuses updated on all plans that moved.
- [ ] Plan portfolio document refreshed.
- [ ] **No-shows logged.** Operator compares the accepts list to the
      actual sign-in. Each no-show recorded in the attendance
      tracker with a date.
- [ ] **First-time no-shows** receive a short, friendly check-in
      from the operator. No judgment. ("We missed you on [date],
      hope everything's OK. Let me know if you'd like to be on the
      list for the next session.")
- [ ] **Repeat no-shows flagged.** Anyone who has now accepted-and-
      not-attended three or more times in the past 12 months goes
      on the captain's list for the conversation described below.

## Within 7 Days

- [ ] Recap published in the chapter's repo / site.
- [ ] Recap email sent to all attendees (with their consent for
      manager cc, if applicable).
- [ ] Recap link posted in the chapter's communication channel.
- [ ] Personal thank-you notes from the captain to two or three
      contributors who did something specific that mattered.
- [ ] Lessons learned filed (3–7 lessons; 1–3 contributed
      upstream).
- [ ] Main SAAF Project organisers notified of any artefacts
      contributed back.
- [ ] Next session's date confirmed in the calendar (if not
      already).
- [ ] Sponsor / endorsing body sent the recap with a one-paragraph
      cover note highlighting what to read.

## Within 14 Days

- [ ] Pull requests for any contributed artefacts opened against
      the main SAAF Project repository.
- [ ] One-on-one meetings scheduled with the three to five
      strongest new contributors from the session (recruitment
      moment).
- [ ] **Repeat no-show conversation.** Captain has a short, kind,
      private conversation with anyone flagged at the 3-day check —
      see [running-hackathons/venue-logistics-and-comfort.md](../../running-hackathons/venue-logistics-and-comfort.md#no-shows-and-repeat-no-shows)
      for the script and the policy. Default outcome: move them to
      "waitlist by default" status, with their consent.

## Within 30 Days

- [ ] Plan portfolio review with co-captain and track leads:
      which plans moved, which are stuck, which to retire.
- [ ] First plan-status update for the next session sent to the
      mailing list.
- [ ] Contributors who soft-claimed a plan but didn't show on the
      day reached out to (no pressure, just check-in).

---

## A Note On Slipping

If you slip past day 7 on the recap, recover as described in
[../../running-hackathons/post-event-recap.md](../../running-hackathons/post-event-recap.md):

- Day 8–14: send the recap anyway, even abbreviated.
- Day 14+: send a half-page note explaining the slip and what's
  coming.
- Don't pretend the slip didn't happen.

The recap discipline is the single most important between-session
discipline. A chapter that consistently misses recaps is a chapter
that loses members faster than it gains them.
