## 10. Conditions Under Which The Chapter Should Pause Or Restructure

The chapter will consider pausing or restructuring if:

- The captain has tried two distinct re-engagement approaches
  without effect.
- Active core falls below three people.
- Endorsing body has not renewed for the year.
- Sponsor or budget has been withdrawn.
- More than two sessions in a row have under-met the success
  criteria above.

## 11. Relationship To The Main SAAF Project And The SAAF Foundation

This chapter federates with the main SAAF Project and is affiliated
with the **SAAF Foundation** under the following terms:

- We adopt the manifesto.
- We qualify the SAAF name appropriately (regional only) and use
  the SAAF name and logo under the SAAF Foundation's brand
  guidelines.
- We acknowledge that the SAAF name, the "SAAF Project" name, and
  the SAAF logo are owned by the SAAF Foundation, and that our use
  of them depends on continued Foundation affiliation.
- We follow the main project's repository structure for
  contributions.
- We commit to attending the annual organiser sync.
- We acknowledge the main project organisers' authority over the
  manifesto, and the Foundation's authority over the marks.

In return, the main project (in coordination with the Foundation)
commits to listing this chapter on the SAAF Project website and
including this chapter in the federation.
