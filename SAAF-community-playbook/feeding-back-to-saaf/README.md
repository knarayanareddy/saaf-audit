# Feeding Back To SAAF

How learnings, artefacts, and outcomes flow from a chapter back to
the main SAAF Project.

The federation model only works if the flow back is real. A chapter
that takes from the community and never contributes is, eventually,
a chapter that the community stops investing in. A chapter that
contributes regularly becomes the channel through which a whole
region or organisation participates in the wider SAAF ecosystem.

This section is for both internal and regional chapters. Internal
chapters typically share *categories of artefacts* (prompts, control
mappings, schema patterns) rather than the artefacts themselves;
regional chapters typically share artefacts in full.

## In this section

- **[contribution-flow.md](contribution-flow.md)** — How an artefact
  produced inside a chapter gets into the main SAAF Project
  repository. The git workflow, the review path, the attribution
  rules.
- **[lessons-learned.md](lessons-learned.md)** — How to write a
  lesson learned that another chapter can use. The format the
  main SAAF Project consumes.
- **[cross-chapter-sync.md](cross-chapter-sync.md)** — The annual
  organiser sync, the quarterly captain calls, and how chapters
  share without overhead.
- **[governance-and-ip.md](governance-and-ip.md)** — IP, attribution,
  what's shared and what's withheld, and how disputes get resolved.

## What to read first

If your chapter has just produced its first contribution-worthy
artefact, read [contribution-flow.md](contribution-flow.md) and the
relevant section of [governance-and-ip.md](governance-and-ip.md).

If you are early in the year and thinking about how to share
back at all, read this README and
[cross-chapter-sync.md](cross-chapter-sync.md).
