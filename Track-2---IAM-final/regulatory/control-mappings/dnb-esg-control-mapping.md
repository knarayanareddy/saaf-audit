# DNB ESG Risk Management — Control Mapping

This document maps DNB supervisory expectations on ESG and climate risk management to auditable controls. It covers the DNB Good Practice on Climate-related Risk Management and DNB's broader sustainable finance supervisory expectations.

---

## 1. DNB Good Practice — Climate-related Risk Management

DNB published its Good Practice document following its thematic review of climate risk management at Dutch financial institutions. The document identifies four elements that institutions are expected to address.

| Element | Topic | Auditable control | Risk if absent | SAAF prompt reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Governance | Board-level responsibility for climate risk explicitly assigned; climate risk agenda item in board and risk committee meetings | High — no accountable owner for climate risk at senior level | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 1 | Governance | Climate risk expertise present at board and management level (training records, external expertise consulted) | Medium — board unable to adequately challenge climate risk assessments | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 2 | Strategy | Climate risk integrated in strategic planning and business model analysis (including scenario analysis over 3–10 year horizon) | High — long-term viability of business model not assessed under climate scenarios | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 2 | Strategy | Material climate-related risks and opportunities identified and reflected in business strategy documents | Medium — strategy not adjusted for foreseeable climate-related disruptions | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 3 | Risk management | Climate risks (transition and physical) identified, assessed, and included in the risk register | High — climate risks not managed within the institution's risk framework | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 3 | Risk management | Climate risk stress test or scenario analysis performed and results used in risk appetite and capital planning | High — capital position not resilient to climate stress scenarios | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 3 | Risk management | Concentration risk in climate-sensitive sectors (e.g. real estate, energy, agriculture) monitored and reported | Medium — portfolio concentration in high-transition-risk sectors not visible | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4 | Disclosure | Climate-related disclosures published in line with TCFD framework and applicable Dutch/EU requirements | Medium — public disclosures incomplete or inconsistent with internal risk assessments | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4 | Disclosure | Internal and external reporting on climate risk is consistent, accurate, and reviewed by a second line function | Medium — greenwashing risk or regulatory reporting errors | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |

## 2. DNB Supervisory Expectations — Sustainable Finance (Broader)

| Topic | Auditable control | Risk if absent | SAAF prompt reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESG in investment policy (insurers, pension funds) | ESG risk factors integrated in investment policy and manager selection criteria | High — ESG risks not considered in investment decisions | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| SFDR compliance (asset managers) | Sustainability-related disclosures on financial products are accurate, complete, and reviewed | High — greenwashing risk, regulatory sanction | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| Biodiversity risk | Biodiversity and nature-related risks identified and assessed as part of environmental risk management | Medium — emerging regulatory expectation; proactive identification reduces future compliance risk | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| Data governance for ESG | ESG data used in risk management and disclosures is subject to data quality controls and independent validation | Medium — ESG risk assessments and disclosures based on unreliable data | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |

---

## Notes

- DNB Good Practice applies to all institutions under DNB prudential supervision (banks, insurers, pension funds). Scope may differ per institution type.
- DNB follows ECB guidance for banks under SSM supervision — cross-reference ECB Guide expectations where applicable.
- DNB has signalled increasing attention to biodiversity and nature-related risks (TNFD framework). Include in scope for forward-looking audits.
- DNB supervisory letters and thematic review outcomes are published on dnb.nl — monitor for updates annually.
- For pension funds, cross-reference IORP II requirements and DNB pension sector guidance alongside this mapping.
