# EBA ESG Risk Management — Control Mapping

This document maps key EBA requirements on ESG risk management to auditable controls. It covers three EBA instruments most relevant for internal audit fieldwork at financial institutions.

---

## 1. EBA Guidelines on Management of ESG Risks (EBA/GL/2024)

| Section | Topic | Auditable control | Risk if absent | SAAF prompt reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | ESG risk identification | Institution has performed a structured ESG risk identification covering transition, physical, and social risks across all material portfolios | High — material ESG risks not identified | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4.2 | ESG risk assessment and measurement | Quantitative or qualitative ESG risk metrics defined and reported in management information | High — ESG risks not measurable or monitored | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4.3 | Integration into risk management framework | ESG risks integrated into the overall risk management framework (credit, market, operational, liquidity) | High — ESG risks managed in silo, not embedded in existing frameworks | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4.4 | ICAAP and ILAAP integration | ESG risks assessed within ICAAP and ILAAP with materiality determination documented | High — capital and liquidity adequacy not adjusted for ESG risks | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4.5 | Transition plans | Institution has a credible, board-approved transition plan with milestones and KPIs | High — no roadmap to manage climate transition risk | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4.6 | Data and methodologies | ESG risk data sourced from reliable providers; data gaps documented with remediation plan | Medium — ESG risk assessments based on incomplete or unreliable data | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4.7 | Scenario analysis | Forward-looking ESG scenario analysis performed covering short, medium, and long-term horizons | High — long-term ESG risks not reflected in strategic planning | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4.8 | Internal governance | ESG risk governance structure defined with clear roles, responsibilities, and escalation paths | Medium — accountability gaps in ESG risk oversight | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |

## 2. EBA Pillar 3 ESG Disclosures (EBA/GL/2022/03)

| Requirement | Topic | Auditable control | Risk if absent | SAAF prompt reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Template 1 | Banking book — climate transition risk | Quantitative disclosure of exposures to carbon-intensive sectors with alignment to Paris Agreement | High — supervisory disclosure obligation not met | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| Template 5 | Banking book — physical climate risk | Exposure by geography and physical risk category (flood, heat, drought) disclosed | High — physical risk concentration not transparent | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| Template 9 | Mitigating actions — climate | Green asset ratio (GAR) and banking book taxonomy alignment ratio disclosed and auditable | Medium — greenwashing risk if disclosures not substantiated | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| Qualitative | Governance and strategy disclosures | Board-level ESG governance described accurately and consistently with internal documents | Medium — inconsistency between public disclosure and internal governance | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |

## 3. EBA Guidelines on Loan Origination and Monitoring (ESG Integration)

| Section | Topic | Auditable control | Risk if absent | SAAF prompt reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ESG integration — origination | Credit underwriting | ESG risk factors included in credit application assessment for corporate borrowers above materiality threshold | High — climate transition risk not priced into credit decisions | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| ESG integration — monitoring | Ongoing credit monitoring | ESG risk indicators monitored post-origination; deterioration triggers review or watch-list classification | Medium — climate risk events not detected in existing portfolio | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| Data collection | Counterparty ESG data | Process in place to collect ESG data from borrowers (energy label, emissions, sector classification) | Medium — ESG risk assessment relies on proxies rather than actual data | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |

## Notes

- EBA/GL/2024 is the primary instrument for ESG risk management governance. Cross-reference with ECB Guide expectations for overlapping requirements (especially expectations 6, 7, 8, 12).
- Pillar 3 ESG disclosure requirements apply to large institutions first (CRR Article 449a). Verify applicability threshold for the institution under audit.
- Check EBA website for updated implementation timelines and Q&A on EBA/GL/2024 — phased application dates may affect scope.
- Green Asset Ratio (GAR) and BTAR calculations require EU Taxonomy alignment data — verify data quality controls separately.
