# ECB Guide on Climate-related and Environmental Risks (2020) — Control Mapping

This document maps the 13 ECB supervisory expectations to auditable controls for internal audit fieldwork. Use this mapping to identify which controls to test for each expectation.

| Expectation | Area | Auditable control | Risk if absent | SAAF prompt reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business environment and strategy | Board-approved climate risk strategy with measurable targets and time horizons | High — no strategic direction for ESG risk management | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 2 | Business environment and strategy | Board minutes and management reporting evidence that climate risks are discussed at senior level | Medium — climate risks not escalated to decision-makers | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 3 | Governance and risk appetite | Climate and environmental risks explicitly included in the Risk Appetite Statement (RAS) with quantitative limits | High — no binding limits on climate risk exposure | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 4 | Governance and risk appetite | Organisational structure with clear ownership of climate risk (e.g. dedicated ESG risk function or CRO mandate) | Medium — accountability gaps in ESG risk oversight | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 5 | Governance and risk appetite | Board and management body have adequate ESG/climate risk expertise (training records, external hires, board profiles) | Medium — insufficient competence to challenge ESG risk assessments | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 6 | Risk management — general | Climate and environmental risks integrated into the ICAAP and ILAAP risk inventory | High — material risks not captured in capital and liquidity adequacy assessment | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 7 | Credit risk | ESG/climate risk criteria included in credit underwriting standards and loan origination policy | High — climate transition risk not reflected in credit decisions | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 8 | Credit risk | Portfolio-level climate risk assessment performed (e.g. sector heatmap, physical risk exposure by geography) | High — no overview of climate risk concentration in the loan book | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 9 | Credit risk | Client ESG data collected and used in credit monitoring and review processes | Medium — climate risk not monitored after loan origination | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 10 | Operational risk | Physical climate risks (e.g. flooding, extreme heat) assessed for own operations and outsourced functions | Medium — business continuity plans do not cover climate events | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 11 | Market and liquidity risk | Transition risk assessed for market and treasury portfolios (e.g. stranded asset exposure, green bond holdings) | Medium — climate risk not reflected in market risk framework | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 12 | Stress testing | Climate risk scenarios included in internal stress testing programme (transition and physical risk scenarios) | High — capital adequacy not tested under climate stress scenarios | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |
| 13 | Disclosure | Climate and environmental risk disclosures published in line with supervisory expectations (Pillar 3, TCFD, CSRD where applicable) | Medium — disclosures incomplete or not aligned with supervisory expectations | `prompts/audit-use-cases/ia-fieldwork-preparation-esg.md` |

## Notes

- This mapping covers the 13 expectations from the ECB Guide on climate-related and environmental risks (November 2020). Check the ECB website for any updated guidance or follow-up supervisory letters.
- Risk ratings are indicative. Calibrate to your institution's risk appetite and materiality thresholds.
- Cross-reference with EBA Guidelines on management of ESG risks (EBA/GL/2024) for overlapping requirements.
- Physical vs. transition risk distinction: expectations 8, 10, and 12 cover both; flag this distinction in fieldwork planning.
