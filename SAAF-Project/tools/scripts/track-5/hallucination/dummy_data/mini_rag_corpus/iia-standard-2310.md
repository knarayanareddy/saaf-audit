# IIA Standard 2310 — Identifying information

**Standard 2310 — Identifying Information**

*Internal auditors must identify sufficient, reliable, relevant, and useful information to achieve the engagement's objectives.*

## Interpretation (IIA Implementation Guidance)

**Sufficient information** is factual, adequate, and convincing so that a prudent, informed person would reach the same conclusions as the auditor.

**Reliable information** is the best attainable information through the use of appropriate engagement techniques.

**Relevant information** supports engagement observations and recommendations and is consistent with the objectives for the engagement.

**Useful information** helps the organisation meet its goals.

## Application to AI-generated findings

When internal audit uses AI tools to generate findings:
- The auditor remains responsible for ensuring the information meets the sufficient/reliable/relevant/useful criteria.
- AI-generated claims must be traceable to source evidence. A conclusion in the workpaper without a link to the evidence that supports it fails Standard 2310.
- Validation of AI output — including hallucination detection — is part of the auditor's due professional care (see also Standard 1220).

Findings that cite standards, clauses, or thresholds must be validated against the source. Fabricated citations (e.g. non-existent clause numbers) fail Standard 2310 because the information is neither reliable nor relevant.
