# A5 — Portal Experience & Best Interaction

**For:** Anyone — especially people **new to SAAF** or with an analytics/UX eye · **Team size:** 3–4 · **Time:** 3 hours

## Goal
Stress-test the **SAAF portal itself** as if you were a newcomer, and answer the big question: **how should everyone best interact with SAAF** — through the portal, Google Drive, GitHub, or some mix? Hand in concrete frictions and a clear recommendation.

## Why it matters
The portal exists to make contributing easy — but not everyone uses it, and guides get skipped. Your honest experience tells us what to simplify and which channel people should actually use. This directly shapes how the *next* sessions run.

## What to do
1. **Walk through it as a newcomer (60 min).** Try to: sign in, set your profile/role, find a plan, claim something, read a guide, and understand what you're supposed to do. Note every moment you hesitate, get confused, or give up. Take screenshots.
2. **Log the frictions (40 min).** Write **≥5 concrete frictions** — each with: where it happened, what you expected, what actually happened, and how bad it is (blocker / annoying / minor).
3. **Compare the channels (40 min).** Honestly compare **Portal vs Google Drive vs GitHub** for the things people actually do: reading assignments, handing in work, finding others' work, tracking progress. What is each genuinely best at?
4. **Recommend the interaction model (20 min).** State clearly how you think everyone should interact going forward, and the **top-3 changes** that would make participation easiest. Be specific.

## Inline reference — what to evaluate
Onboarding (first 5 minutes) · finding your assignment · claiming a plan/role · reading guides · handing in work · seeing progress / leaderboard · navigation & wording · mobile vs laptop. **Friction columns:** Where | Expected | Actual | Severity (blocker/annoying/minor) | Suggested fix.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] A newcomer walkthrough (with screenshots where useful)
- [ ] **≥5 frictions** logged with severity + suggested fix
- [ ] Portal vs Drive vs GitHub comparison
- [ ] A clear recommended interaction model + top-3 changes

## SMART
**S** the portal + interaction model · **M** ≥5 frictions + 1 recommendation · **A** no coding · **R** decides how the program runs next · **T** 3 hours.

## Suggested time-box
60 walkthrough · 40 frictions · 40 channel comparison · 20 recommendation · 20 write-up & hand in.
